Privacy Policy for VoltTest

Application: VoltTest: 90s Fast Battery Test
Developer / Brand: Mircasoft (https://mircasoft.com)
Last Updated: September 3, 2026
Summary: VoltTest is built with a 100% on-device local privacy approach. Your hardware benchmark measurements, battery telemetry curves, and test history are processed exclusively on your device and are never sent to external servers.

1. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

A. Hardware & Battery Telemetry (Processed Locally Only)

During the 90-second benchmark test, VoltTest accesses real-time hardware telemetry from Android system services (BatteryManager, ActivityManager, StatFs, TrafficStats):

  • Battery Telemetry: Voltage (mV), temperature (°C), instantaneous amperage (mA), battery level percentage, and charging state.
  • Hardware Metrics: CPU load percentage, RAM usage, storage availability, and network throughput rate.

Purpose: To calculate your battery voltage stability score, thermal dissipation curve, and save historical benchmark trends on your device.
Storage: Stored strictly in local device storage (SharedPreferences). This data is never transmitted to any third party or remote server.

B. Diagnostics and Crash Reporting (Third-Party SDKs)

To ensure app stability and diagnose runtime crashes across diverse Android hardware configurations, we use trusted Google Firebase services:

  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymous stack traces, device model, operating system version, and crash timestamp when an unexpected application error occurs.
  • Google Firebase Analytics: Collects basic aggregate, non-personally identifiable app open and benchmark completion statistics to help improve application reliability.

These services operate under Google's Privacy Policy and data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal identity information (Name, Email, Phone number, Address).
  • No location data (GPS or coarse location).
  • No user accounts, credentials, or contacts.
  • No photos, media, files, or sensitive hardware identifiers (IMEI, MAC Address).

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

  • WAKE_LOCK: Keeps CPU active during the 60-second stress phase so background throttling does not distort benchmark accuracy.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Ensures the benchmark diagnostic service is not terminated by Android battery optimization while running.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Notifies you when a 90-second benchmark is completed and provides periodic test reminders if enabled.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Re-schedules periodic battery health check reminders following device restart.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

All test history and preferences are stored exclusively on your device. You can permanently delete all stored benchmark records at any time directly within the app (Settings / History clear option) or by clearing the app data / uninstalling the app from Android Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

VoltTest does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

6. Security

We value your trust in providing your device with local diagnostics. All diagnostic transmission to Firebase is encrypted using modern TLS/HTTPS encryption protocols.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with an updated revision date.
